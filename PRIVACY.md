# syncme privacy policy

syncme doesn't collect, send or sell any data.

when you click copy on a site, the extension reads that site's cookies and passes them to the syncme bridge running on your own computer. the bridge saves them into a separate chrome profile on that same computer. nothing leaves your machine and there's no server.

the extension only reads cookies for sites you added yourself. the list of saved sites and the auto-copy setting are kept in chrome's local extension storage.

no analytics, no tracking, no third parties.

questions: open an issue at https://github.com/levinkin/syncme/issues
